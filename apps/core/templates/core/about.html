公司简介Company Profile

新帆航空（Xinfan Airlines）是一家成立于 Roblox 平台的虚拟航空公司，致力于为 Roblox 玩家提供真实、专业的虚拟航空体验。我们模拟真实航空公司的运营模式，提供定期航班、机组人员招募、里程积累等服务。Xinfan Airlines is a virtual airline on the Roblox platform, dedicated to providing authentic and professional virtual aviation experiences. We simulate real airline operations with scheduled flights, crew recruitment, and mileage programs.

无论是想体验飞行员驾驶乐趣的玩家，还是想要担任空乘、地勤的航空爱好者，都可以在新帆航空找到适合自己的角色。Whether you want to experience life as a pilot or serve as cabin crew or ground staff, Xinfan has a role for every aviation enthusiast.

蓝天联盟 SKY LINKSKY LINK Alliance

新帆航空是蓝天联盟的成员航空公司之一。 Xinfan Airlines is a member airline of the SKY LINK alliance.

蓝天联盟（SKY LINK）成立于 2022 年 9 月 30 日，旨在联结 Roblox 航空，其成员航空公司合力协作，提供一致、超卓、完美的旅行体验。飞行常客享有特别的优待和奖励，包括在整个联盟网络内赢取和兑换里程和积分。联盟会员享有机场贵宾休息室的使用权，还可在某些机场使用快速安检通道。 SKY LINK was founded on 30 September 2022 to connect airlines across Roblox. Its member airlines work together to deliver a consistent, outstanding and seamless travel experience. Frequent flyers enjoy special privileges and rewards, including the ability to earn and redeem miles and points across the entire alliance network. Alliance members also have access to airport lounges, and can use fast-track security at selected airports.
